sec. 02 — systems built

Marketing infrastructure

Each of these started as a bottleneck — a spreadsheet that broke, a location nobody could find on Maps, leads going cold in a WhatsApp thread. They're built to be used by non-technical showroom staff, not just by me.

LeadForge

Problem: lead activity for three brands across 22 locations lived in disconnected spreadsheets and WhatsApp threads, with no accountability or lifecycle visibility.

Built: a Flask/PostgreSQL CRM, now on its 40th-plus iteration, with canonical lead IDs per brand, Meta and WhatsApp Business API integration, automated Google Sheets reconciliation, role-based assignment, and a peer sync between a local instance and a Render deployment.

Result: 826+ leads under active, trackable management; 700+ processed through the structured workflow; ~600 previously unattended Meta leads recovered; response time cut to under 30 minutes.

Digital Asset Operations

Problem: marketing assets from 22 locations had no central tracking — files were lost, duplicated, or never made it from the showroom floor to a published post.

Built: a Google Apps Script intake and lifecycle logger with automatic categorisation, publication-status tracking, and daily reconciliation, designed for zero-training use.

Result: 198+ assets tracked end-to-end with full lifecycle visibility, replacing what had been an untracked, manual process.

Google Apps ScriptGoogle Sheets API

Local Presence & GBP Framework

Problem: only 2 of 22 dealership locations had a working, accurate Google Business Profile — most local search traffic was invisible.

Built: a repeatable verification and optimisation workflow run through direct field visits across multiple Uttar Pradesh districts, a Review Tracker (Apps Script + SerpApi) benchmarking ratings against competitors, a Map SEO Gap Report, and a weekly Search Console tracker.

Result: 17 locations verified and fully operational, materially expanding local search and Maps visibility.

Google Business ProfileApps ScriptSerpApiSearch Console

WhatsApp Campaign Infrastructure

Problem: win-back, service, and insurance follow-ups needed to reach segmented customers directly, with a reliable record of every message delivered.

Built: a Gupshup webhook logger writing straight to Google Sheets, plus a Campaign Console for building and sending win-back, service, and insurance broadcasts to segmented lists with A/B tested messaging.

Result: 50,000+ segmented contacts under management, with number provisioning and broadcast delivery running end-to-end.
